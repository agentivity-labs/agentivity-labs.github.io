Agentivity Labs

Open source projects & research

Agentivity Labs is our open research and engineering lab — where we explore what it means to build production-grade interfaces for AI agents.

Our mission is to make agentic AI accessible to everyone — from individuals exploring what agents can do, to teams running complex workflows, to large organisations embedding agents into their core operations. Not just accessible to engineers: accessible to anyone who wants to work alongside AI agents, understand what they are doing, and stay in control.

We believe the bottleneck is not model capability — it is the lack of a mature ecosystem around it. The open-source work published here is part of that ecosystem: the building blocks we open up so the broader community can build on the same foundation.
